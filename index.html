The whole picture.

I work across the visual and technical sides of a website, keeping the original idea present through every decision.

Understand what matters.

The people, the purpose, the next step. I start by understanding what the experience needs to communicate and what someone should be able to do.

Make it unmistakable.

Typography, imagery, space and motion become a visual language built around the project. The identity should feel specific, considered and natural.

Carry the idea into the build.

I develop the responsive frontend, connect the interactions and refine the details across screen sizes. The final experience should work as well as it looks.

I’m a frontend developer and visual designer based in Sarajevo, working independently with people and brands around the world.

My background brings graphic and web design together with frontend development. I’m drawn to expressive typography, carefully composed interfaces and the small moments that make a digital experience feel human.

I stay with a project from the first idea through its implementation. That means the visual direction and the technical decisions can develop together — with a direct conversation between you and the person doing the work.
