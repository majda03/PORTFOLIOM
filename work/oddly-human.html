The toolsReact · TypeScript · Editorial design

The contextIndependent concept · 2026

The intention.

The small, familiar things we do without knowing why can become a compelling editorial world. Oddly Human starts with recognition, then invites the reader to explore.

The direction.

I built a vivid editorial identity around expressive type, original illustrations and unexpected compositions. Each interactive feature supports the story being told, from thought loops to the ways we present ourselves to others.

The experience.

Nine stories, topic navigation, full-text search, bookmarks and private reflections, alongside five interactive editorial features. Reading content is rendered into static pages for direct access.
