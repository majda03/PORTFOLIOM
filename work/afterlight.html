AFTERLIGHT

An object. An atmosphere. A little obsession.

A project by Majda Kapetanović

A digital light atelier where a sculptural lamp unfolds into a study of form, material and atmosphere.

My role
Visual direction, interface design & frontend development
The tools
React · TypeScript · Three.js
Open live experience

The intention

Light changes how a place feels. I wanted that sensation to become the subject of a website: an object that reveals its character through movement, lighting and space.

The direction

A continuous visual narrative moves from the sculptural object to its material, its surroundings and the light you compose. Large typography and carefully paced transitions give each transformation room to register.

The experience

A procedural 3D light study, scroll-led staging, material and light controls, and a personal light collection. An illustrated fallback keeps the experience available when WebGL is not supported.
