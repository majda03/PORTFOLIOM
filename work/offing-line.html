THE WORK / 2026

The idea

Private charter involves many decisions: where to travel, which vessel to consider, and how to communicate personal preferences. This independent concept explores how to make those choices feel connected and easy to understand.

The approach

I brought destinations, vessel choices and preferences into a coherent five-section experience. A visitor can explore a passage, review their choices and prepare an enquiry rather than switching between disconnected forms.

What I built

A responsive React interface with careful typography, motion, stateful choices and a clear final review. The experience preserves decisions while moving through the journey.

Independent fictional concept. No vessels are represented as available; prepared enquiries are not sent and no booking is made.
