ROYAL COSMETICS

Beautiful on the surface. Considered underneath.

My role

Visual direction, interface design
& frontend development

The tools

React · TypeScript · Commerce

Open the live experience

The intention.

A storefront needs more than a beautiful first screen. This independent commerce concept connects product discovery to the everyday details of browsing, saving and checking out.

The direction.

A clear visual hierarchy supports browsing while the frontend keeps cart, wishlist and product state coherent across the journey.

The experience.

Search and filtering, persistent cart and wishlist, stock-aware quantity controls, validated simulated checkout, and route-based React views. This is a demonstration storefront; it does not take payments.
