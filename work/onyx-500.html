The intention.

A premium product website should let people discover the object. ONYX—500 explores that idea through matte-black studio headphones and a browser-based 3D presentation.

The direction.

The product is the centre of the experience. A dark studio setting, restrained typography and direct interaction focus attention on its shape and character.

The experience.

A responsive product showcase with a WebGL 3D headphone scene. Open the original live experience to explore the model on a browser with WebGL support.
