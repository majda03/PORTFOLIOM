ONYX—500

An object you can explore.

A project by Majda Kapetanović

An interactive, matte-black headphone experience built around a WebGL product scene.

My role
Visual direction, interface design & frontend development
The tools
WebGL · Creative development
Open live experience

The intention

A product website should invite a closer look. ONYX—500 explores how a pair of matte-black studio headphones can become an interactive digital experience.

The direction

A black studio setting and a focused visual language keep attention on the product’s form and the interaction itself.

The experience

A responsive product showcase with an interactive WebGL 3D scene. The original live experience requires a browser with WebGL support.
