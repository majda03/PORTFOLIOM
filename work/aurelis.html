The tools

The intention.

A watch cannot be understood through a single static image. This independent luxury-watch concept explores how a digital visitor can discover its shape, details and construction directly in the browser.

The direction.

I designed a restrained storytelling system where each chapter changes the watch’s presentation. The 3D model becomes part of the narrative, with exploded views and an invitation to look more closely.

The experience.

A procedural Three.js watch, scroll choreography, exploded-view moments, cinematic lighting, interactive 360° inspection and reduced-motion support.
